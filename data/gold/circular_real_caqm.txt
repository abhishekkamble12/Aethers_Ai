Sub: Implementation of directions under Stage-III of Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) in Delhi.

In pursuance of CAQM Direction No. 84 dated 16th January 2025 invoking Stage-III ('Severe' Air Quality) of Graded Response Action Plan in NCR, all Head of Schools (Govt., Govt. Aided, Unaided Private Recognized) under Directorate of Education are hereby directed as under:

1. All classes up to primary level (Nursery to Class V) shall run in hybrid mode (both physical and online options where feasible).
2. All outdoor sports and physical education activities in schools shall remain strictly suspended under Stage III of GRAP.
3. Morning assemblies, if any, shall be conducted strictly indoors within well-ventilated classrooms or halls.
4. Physical education and sports teachers shall conduct indoor wellness sessions or educational discussions during scheduled periods.

This issues with the prior approval of the Competent Authority.
